About

Iran, beyond the postcard.

Iran Eco Trip is an independent travel and storytelling project created to show another side of Iran — its landscapes, history, culture, traditions and everyday life.

From mountains and forests to deserts, villages and centuries-old cities, Iran is a land shaped by extraordinary geographic and cultural diversity.

Iran Eco Trip documents these experiences and shares them with people who are curious to discover Iran beyond the usual narratives.
